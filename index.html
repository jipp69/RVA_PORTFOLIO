Artist Statement

​ As a student of Information Technology, I have always perceived the world through the lens of structure, logic, and functional design. However, my journey through the Reading Visual Arts (RVA) course has challenged me to look beyond the "code" of an image and explore the emotional and cultural narratives that visual media can convey. This portfolio is a curated collection of my growth, representing a transition from a purely analytical mindset to one that embraces creative expression.

​ In these modules, I explored how the seven elements of art serve as the fundamental building blocks of communication—much like syntax in a programming language. From the rhythmic complexity of Filipino-inspired patterns to the storytelling power of photo narratives, I have learned that art is not merely an aesthetic endeavor but a vital tool for human connection. My work often reflects a minimalist and professional aesthetic, aiming for clarity and balance. By integrating my technical background with artistic principles, I strive to create visuals that are both purposeful and evocative, proving that the digital and creative worlds are not separate, but are two sides of the same communicative coin.
